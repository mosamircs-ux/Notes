NoteFlow Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.mosamir.noteflow

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

NoteFlow is a note-taking and checklist application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use NoteFlow.

2. Your Notes & Local Storage

All text notes, checklists, app lock settings, and backup files you create in NoteFlow are stored locally on your device. NoteFlow does not operate user accounts and does not upload, sync, or transmit the contents of your notes to our servers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep NoteFlow free, the app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain non-note device and usage data to serve, measure, and personalize ads (where permitted by your consent choices), including:

  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID) and device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interactions, impressions, and app launch / crash diagnostics

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, NoteFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and respect your advertising consent preferences. Learn more about how Google uses data at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to load advertisements via Google AdMob and check consent status.
  • AD_ID: Used by Google AdMob in accordance with Android advertising ID policies and your device settings.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / WAKE_LOCK: Used strictly if you enable scheduled local automatic backups of your notes.
  • Storage / File Picker: Used only when you explicitly choose to import or export note files or JSON backups.

5. Data Deletion & Control

Because your notes are stored locally on your device, you can delete any note or clear all app data at any time from within the app or via Android Settings → Apps → NoteFlow → Storage → Clear data. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Children's Privacy

NoteFlow is a general-audience productivity application and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the NoteFlow Google Play Store page.
