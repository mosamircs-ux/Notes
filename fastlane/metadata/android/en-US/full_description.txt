NoteFlow is a fast, modern Material 3 note-taking and checklist app built to keep your ideas, tasks, and daily plans organized without clutter.

✨ MODERN MATERIAL 3 DESIGN
Write inside clean, rounded surface cards with an Indigo & Soft Slate aesthetic crafted for effortless reading in both Light and Dark themes.

📝 SMART TEXT NOTES
• Instant automatic saving so your ideas are never lost
• Full undo/redo history, live word counter, and in-note search
• Link notes to local text files, share, print, or export anytime

✅ EFFORTLESS CHECKLISTS
• Create shopping lists, daily routines, and project to-dos
• Drag-and-drop reordering, custom sorting, and collapsible completed items

🔒 PRIVATE & SECURE
• Lock individual notes or the entire app with PIN, pattern, or biometrics
• All notes are stored locally on your device
• Export/import JSON backups and schedule automatic backups

🖼️ CUSTOMIZABLE HOME WIDGETS
• Pin your favorite note or checklist straight to your home screen with customizable colors and typography

🔓 OPEN-SOURCE TRANSPARENCY
